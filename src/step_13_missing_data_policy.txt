Step 13 missing-data policy

The aligned panels retain all dates observed in at least one commodity.
For each commodity, isolated one-row missing observations are forward-filled
using the immediately preceding available settlement price. These generally
reflect differing market holiday calendars, such as the U.S. closure for
Martin Luther King Jr. Day on 17 January 2005.

Missing runs longer than one observation are not filled. They remain missing
and are retained for investigation, preventing stale prices from being carried
over a potentially material data outage or unavailable contract period.

The original outer-joined panels are retained unchanged. Separate cleaned
panels and quality-control files record missing values before and after
cleaning.
